# Editorial guidelines

Use these as guardrails, not as a gate before writing.

- Put the lived experience before the technology explanation.
- Be specific about what is remembered, what is documented, and what is uncertain.
- Do not invent dialogue or present reconstructed scenes as recordings.
- Treat real people with care; anonymize or omit identifying details when needed.
- Explain unfamiliar technical terms in ordinary language.
- Keep the reflective tone open and grounded. Let listeners draw their own conclusion.
- The Persian script is the primary performance text. The English version should carry the meaning and voice naturally, not translate word for word.
- Add source links for historical claims that shape the story. Personal memories can be identified as recollection.
