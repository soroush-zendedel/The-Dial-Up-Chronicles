## One-sentence premise

A young person in Zabol enters Yahoo chat rooms and discovers that finding a connection can mean learning a new shared language, including through popular novels.

## Central question

How did the desire to connect shape what a young person chose to learn?

## Why this story

The memory connects the early web's social world to the curiosity and problem-solving that later shaped the narrator's work.

## Opening scene or image

To be chosen from a specific remembered moment before scripting.

## Key beats

- The first remembered entry into a Yahoo chat room.
- Reading the room and noticing what made conversation possible.
- The move from the Shahnameh toward popular novels, and what that meant to the narrator at the time.
- A reflection connecting the need for a shared language to later learning and making.
