- **Version:** v1

## Prompt

Create cover art for The Dial-Up Chronicles, a reflective documentary podcast about personal memories of the early web, told from Zabol. The mood is quiet and nocturnal, like a computer screen glowing in a dark room. Use a restrained composition that remains legible as a small square thumbnail. Suggest dial-up-era internet through subtle visual cues rather than clutter or generic retro collage. Do not include logos, interface screenshots, or text unless explicitly added in a separate design pass.
