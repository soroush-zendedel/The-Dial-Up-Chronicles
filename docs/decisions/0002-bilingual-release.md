# Decision 0002: Bilingual pilot release

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Decision

Write each episode in Persian first. Once the Persian source script is stable, prepare a separate English adaptation that preserves the story and meaning while sounding natural when spoken. The pilot will be recorded and released in both languages as a test.

Publish each language version as its own YouTube podcast playlist, with language-specific narration, video edit, title, description, and captions. Reuse screen recordings and other visuals when suitable, but align each edit to its narration. Manual video uploads do not require RSS.

After the pilot, review audience response and the added production effort before deciding whether to make English releases part of the regular schedule.

## Why

The English version can reach listeners beyond the Persian-speaking audience, while a Persian-first writing process preserves the narrator's natural voice and keeps one script as the source of truth. Separate playlists make each show's language clear to listeners.

## Practical rule

Do not translate or produce every episode in two languages by default. The pilot is the test; decide what to continue from its results and the workload it creates.
