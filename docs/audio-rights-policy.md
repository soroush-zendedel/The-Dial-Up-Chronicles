# Audio and rights policy

Only include audio in the repository when its license and source are clear and its size is practical. Never commit audio without permission or a license that covers the intended use. Cleared audio files stored under `docs/` can also be served by the site.

For each music or sound asset used, keep a short record with:

- Asset name and whether it is licensed, original, or generated.
- Creator/source and license or permission reference.
- Generation tool or agent and version, if applicable.
- Prompt and generation date, or a link to the versioned prompt record.
- Episodes using the asset.
- Where the approved media file is stored, if it is kept outside Git.

Generated does not automatically mean cleared. Check the tool's applicable terms and retain the relevant evidence. If rights are unclear, do not use the asset in a release.

The episode cue sheet records where and how a sound is used. Shared assets have one central record under `assets/music/` or `assets/sound-effects/` to avoid duplicate histories.
